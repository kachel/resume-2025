Learning in Public

August 18, 2026

Over the course of this summer there have been a series of rolling brown and black outs at my home. I can see first hand how the grid is struggling to keep up with this new energy hungry world. Be it higher temps or data centers, there is a need for more electricity. This got me thinking about the grid and energy storage. I realized that I have not really thought about chemistry or physics since I was in high school. How better to understand the grid than by relearning the science behind it? This is the reason that I am putting my science notes out there and learning in public. In the hopes that I can also help tackle this very real problem. Here is my three week streak!!
